Creator Program

Are You Eligible?

Our agency accepts South Asian creators ready to take their content to the next level. Here's what we look for:

01

Follower Count

You have 5K–100K+ followers on major platforms like Instagram or TikTok. Micro and macro influencers welcome.

02

Consistent Traction

Your content shows consistently good engagement — views, likes, shares — demonstrating an active, invested audience.

03

Content Creator

You actively create and publish content on a regular basis and are committed to growing your platform.

04

Ready to Partner

You're open to brand collaborations and excited to represent companies that align with your personal brand and values.

Joining is always 100% FREE — just sign up and we'll follow up with you.
